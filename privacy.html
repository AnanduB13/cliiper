Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Cliiper is a personal, self-hosted tool. It runs only on its developer's own computer and is used only to publish the developer's own video clips to the developer's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public.

Information Cliiper accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl, Cliiper uploads videos, sets a video's title, description and privacy status, and reads the connected channel's ID and name so it can show which channel is connected.
  • Instagram: through Meta's official Instagram API, Cliiper publishes Reels to the connected professional account and reads its account ID, username and post links.

Cliiper does not read email, contacts, files, viewing history, comments or any other personal data.

How information is stored

Access tokens and the upload queue are stored only on the developer's private computer, in files readable only by that user. They are never sent to any server other than Google's and Meta's own APIs.

Sharing

Cliiper does not sell, share, rent or transfer any data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and contains no analytics or tracking. Cliiper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected in Cliiper, which deletes them. Access can also be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or Instagram's apps and websites settings.

YouTube API Services

Cliiper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on Cliiper's Google sign-in screen.

Cliiper

A personal, self-hosted tool that turns long stream and podcast videos into short vertical clips.

What it does

  • Finds the strongest moments in a long video and renders them as 9:16 clips with captions.
  • Lets its owner schedule those clips and post them to their own YouTube channel and Instagram account at chosen times.

Who it is for

Cliiper is run privately by its developer on their own computer. It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and it is not offered to other users. The Google sign-in exists only so the developer can authorise uploads to their own YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube access

Cliiper uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their visibility. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is stored.
